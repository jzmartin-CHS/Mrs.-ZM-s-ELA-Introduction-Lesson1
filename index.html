Step 1

🧠 Pre-Assessment

Answer before reviewing the expectations. This is just a baseline—do your best.

Step 2

🎒 Come Prepared

Every day, bring:

  • Fully charged Chromebook
  • Notebook
  • Folder
  • Pen or pencil
Phones are not allowed in class per school policy.
Step 3

🔔 Start Strong & Participate

  1. Be in class—not roaming the halls.
  2. Be seated at the start of the class period.
  3. Begin your bellringer.
  4. Participate in class, including discussions and group activities.
Participation matters: Not participating may result in a zero on the assignment or disciplinary action.

🚪 Exit Tickets

  1. Complete the daily Exit Ticket.
  2. Show Mrs. ZM your Exit Ticket sheet before leaving.
  3. Exit Tickets are graded weekly.
  4. Leave when class is dismissed.
Step 4

📚 Work, Homework & Reading

Assignments & due dates

Schoology is updated Mondays with weekly assignments. If you do not finish an assignment in class, it is homework. Do homework at home, not in another teacher's classroom. Each Schoology assignment shows its start date and due date; larger projects may have longer time frames.

Academic honesty: All students are expected to do their own work. Copying another student's assignment is cheating and results in a zero.

Paper copies

Scan or photograph paper work and upload it to Schoology.

Late work

Late assignments are not accepted. If you have extenuating circumstances, talk with Mrs. ZM.

Absences

If you know about an absence or need a make-up test, tell Mrs. ZM or email before the absence. Generally, you have a week from returning to complete missed assignments. For illness, an individualized plan will be developed.

School activity: See Mrs. ZM before the absence. Work is still due the same day unless prior arrangements are made.

📖 Reading homework

Assigned reading is expected to be completed outside of class as we read larger works of literature.

Book rule: If a book comes home, bring it back the next school day.
Step 5

💻 Schoology: Your Class Hub

  • Weekly assignments are updated on Mondays.
  • Assignments are organized by week and date.
  • Check start dates and due dates.
  • Mrs. ZM will identify work that continues across multiple days.
  • The Information and Help Materials topic contains technology tutorials, the syllabus, classroom expectations, and other helpful resources.

Quick Check

Step 6

🏫 Staying in Class & SmartPass

Students are expected to be in class during their assigned class period.

  1. Ask Mrs. ZM first.
  2. If approved, use SmartPass.
  3. Follow school expectations and return promptly.
Important: Do not make the SmartPass first and ask afterward.

🎟️ Before You Leave

Complete your daily Exit Ticket and show your Exit Ticket sheet to Mrs. ZM before leaving the classroom.
